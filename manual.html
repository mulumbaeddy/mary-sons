1. Getting started

Sign in

  1. Open INV SYSTEM and type your email and password.
  2. Press Sign in. Forgot your password? Type your email, then press Forgot password? and follow the email link.
  3. Accounts are created by the owner (in Supabase). There is no public sign-up.

Install it on your phone or computer

  • Android / Chrome: press Install app in the menu (or Chrome ⋮ → Install app).
  • iPhone / Safari: press Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • It then opens like a normal app, with its own icon.

Moving around

On a computer the menu is on the left. On a phone, press the ☰ button (top-left, always visible) to open it. Password changes your password; Sign out leaves the app.

2. How we count: cartons and pieces

The system counts stock in pieces. Your supplier sells in cartons, and each product has a fixed number of pieces in one carton.

Example: a carton of NZIWA — 9X6 holds 100 pieces. You have 250 pieces in stock → the system shows 2 cartons + 50 pieces.
  • You buy mostly in cartons.
  • You sell in pieces or cartons. Your selling price is per piece; selling a carton = pieces × price (you can change the amount on the line).
  • Every product has a code. Two products can share a name, but never a code.

3. Dashboard

Your day at a glance: today's sales, customers who owe you, low-stock items, and recent sales. Partners and the owner also see 30-day profit, stock value and a 14-day sales chart.

4. Products & Stock

Add a product

  1. Open Products & Stock → Add product.
  2. Fill in: Product name, Product code (required, unique), Pieces in one carton, Buying price per carton, Selling price per piece, Low-stock alert (in pieces).
  3. Type the Opening stock (cartons) you already have today, then save.

Other actions

ActionWhat it does
EditChange name, code, prices, alert level. (To change quantity use Adjust stock.)
Adjust stockType the real count after a stock-take and give a reason. It is recorded in the history.
HistoryEvery change to that product: opening, purchases, sales, adjustments.
DeleteRemoves a product and its history. Not allowed while an active sale or purchase uses it — cancel that sale/purchase first.
Export CSVDownload the product list for Excel.
Deleting a product is permanent. If you only want to stop selling it, leave it with zero stock.

5. Purchases (owner & partner)

Record stock you buy from a supplier. Stock goes up automatically.

  1. Purchases → New purchase.
  2. Enter the supplier and date.
  3. Choose the product (shown as Name — CODE), the unit (Cartons or Pieces), the quantity and the cost paid. Use + Add another product for more items.
  4. Enter the amount paid now. Anything unpaid becomes a supplier debt.
  5. Save.

Cancelling a purchase (trash icon) removes it and takes the stock back out.

6. Sales

Record a sale

  1. Open Sales and press New sale. (This button only appears on the Sales page.)
  2. Enter the customer (default Walk-in) and the date.
  3. Pick the product — it shows as Name — CODE — then choose Pieces or Cartons and the quantity. The amount fills in from the selling price; you may change it.
  4. Selling a carton and loose pieces of the same product? Add the product on two lines. The system adds them together on one receipt line, e.g. 1 carton + 2 pieces.
  5. Choose the payment method: Cash, Mobile money or Credit (pay later). For credit you must type the customer's name.
  6. Check Amount received now. The rest is recorded as the customer's debt.
  7. Press Save sale. Stock reduces automatically. You can print the receipt straight away.

Receipts and other actions

  • 👁 View shows the items; Print receipt prints it with product, code, quantity (cartons + pieces), total, paid and balance due.
  • Receive payment on an unpaid sale records money from the customer.
  • Cancel sale (owner only) deletes the sale and returns the stock.
  • Search by sale number or customer; filter by Paid / Partial / Unpaid; Export CSV for Excel.
You cannot sell more than you have. The system warns Not enough stock.

7. Payments & Debts

  • Customer debts — who owes you, how much, since when. Press the ✔ icon to receive a payment.
  • Supplier debts (owner & partner) — what you still owe suppliers. Pay them from here.
  • Payment history — every payment received or paid out.

A payment can be partial. The status moves Unpaid → Partial → Paid automatically.

8. Expenses

Record business costs: Transport, Rent, Utilities, Labour, Packaging, Communication, Other.

  1. Expenses → Add expense.
  2. Choose the date, category, amount and who paid (the business or a person), add a description, and save.

Filter by category to see totals. Only the owner can delete an expense.

9. Reports (owner & partner)

  1. Open Reports and choose a period (this week, this month, or custom dates).
  2. You get: profit summary, cash & balances, best-selling products, expenses by category, and daily sales.
  3. Press print to produce a report you can hand to your partner.

Profit = sales − cost of the goods sold − expenses.

10. Working offline

The app keeps working without internet, like WhatsApp.

  • Open the app with internet once so it can save your data. After that it opens offline.
  • While offline you can record sales, purchases, payments, expenses, new products and stock adjustments. They show as PENDING and stock updates straight away.
  • When internet returns they are sent to the system automatically, in the order you made them. You will see a message when done.
  • If the system refuses an entry (for example, stock ran out because someone else sold it), a red bar appears. Press Review to see which entry and why, then enter it again if needed.
Needs internet: cancelling sales or purchases, deleting products or expenses, and editing products. Don't sign out while entries are still pending.

12. Problems & answers

ProblemWhat to do
"Not enough stock"Check the stock on Products & Stock. Buy more, or fix a wrong count with Adjust stock.
"Cannot be deleted / used in sale…"Cancel the sale or purchase named in the message first, then delete the product.
"Enter the customer name for a credit sale"Credit sales need a real customer name, not Walk-in.
"Code already exists"Each product needs a different code. Add a letter or number to make it unique.
Sent to the login page unexpectedlyYour login expired. Sign in again.
App looks old after an updateClose and reopen it twice.
Red bar after being offlinePress Review, read the reason, and re-enter the entry.
Best habits: record every sale straight away, do a stock count weekly and use Adjust stock, and check Customer debts every week.
